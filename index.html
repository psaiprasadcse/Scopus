Academic year definition: 1 July to 30 June. A paper belongs to the cycle determined by its publisher year/month when available, otherwise its Scopus year/month; neither represents the first Scopus indexing date. †Unknown-month papers cannot be assigned reliably to a July–June cycle; the warning shows records in its two calendar years that need review, not added papers. An incomplete cycle may lack a calendar-year report, include an API retrieval error, or still be in progress. Citations are current cumulative counts for papers in each cycle, not citations received during that cycle. Journal Q1–Q4 uses the report's latest completed CiteScore snapshot, not necessarily the historical quartile.
